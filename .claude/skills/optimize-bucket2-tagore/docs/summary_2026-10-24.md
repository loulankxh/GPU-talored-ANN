  at matching or slightly lower recall; sift100M L64 0.842 vs CAGRA ACE 0.870 / TAGORE 0.862.
- **float32 (msTuring)**: every path now runs (P2) with 0 defects. 1M / 10M are faster than TAGORE (0.76× / 0.56×)
  but below the CAGRA quality floor (msTuring1M itopk64 0.756 vs floor 0.782; msTuring10M 0.717 vs 0.750) →
  P4 calibration (A2: CENTROID_RATIO × NPROBE). msTuring100M: 3918 s = 3.25× CAGRA ACE (1205 s), 1.81× TAGORE;
  L64 0.722 (CAGRA 0.751, TAGORE 0.720). **Step 4 alone is 2451 s** (exact fp32 SGEMM over 400K centroids, quadratic
  in N) — A5 (CAGRA-search assignment above 32768 centroids, as for uint8) awaits the user; Step 2 316 s is next.
- Pre-prune KNN recall@10: SIFT 0.94 / 0.86 / 0.66 (1M / 10M / 100M), msTuring 0.69 / 0.71 / 0.70 — the msTuring
  gap is a bucketing-parameter issue (P4), not size.
- Memory: msTuring100M bucket2 peak RSS 93.8 GB (float records 4× SIFT), GPU 15.2 GB; within the 200 GB budget.
